# Product Vision: ATELIER & ACADÉMIE

## Executive Summary
**ATELIER & ACADÉMIE** bridges the gap between high-fashion couture commerce and premier atelier education. Rather than fracturing customer touchpoints across disparate storefronts, LMS portals, and scheduling tools (e.g. Shopify + Teachable + Calendly), ATELIER & ACADÉMIE binds the three pillars into a single luxury ecosystem:

1. **The Brand (Commerce)**: Customers acquire limited-run ready-to-wear pieces, capsule collections, and bespoke garments.
2. **The Academy (Education)**: Aspiring designers, tailors, and artisans enroll in rigorous digital and in-studio masterclasses, complete with curriculum tracking and verifiable cryptographic certificates.
3. **The Mentorship (Tutoring)**: 1-on-1 private studio and digital critiques with acclaimed instructors, patternmakers, and fashion entrepreneurs.

## Target Personas
- **The Connoisseur Client**: Appreciates artisan tailoring, reviews runway details, purchases garments, tracks fulfillment.
- **The Fashion Student**: Enrolls in technical courses (Pattern Drafting, Haute Couture Embroidery), submits assignments, tracks lesson milestones, claims verified credentials.
- **The Master Instructor**: Conducts atelier workshops, sets slot availability for portfolio reviews and draping critiques, tracks student progress.
- **The Atelier Director / Admin**: Oversees inventory levels, class schedules, instructor roster, revenue analytics, customer orders, and content publishing.

## Brand Experience & Visual Language
- Minimalist luxury aesthetic: warm ecru/off-white (`#FAF9F5`), bespoke dark charcoal (`#1A1A1A`), warm champagne accents (`#C2A676`), and razor-thin hairline dividers.
- High editorial typography: Cormorant Garamond headings paired with crisp Plus Jakarta Sans body prose.
- Zero-pill discipline: Clean typographic separation (`·`, `/`) instead of tacky SaaS badge pills.
